# OpenDrive — Status

*Snapshot as of 2026-09-29. This file covers what is built and what the
standing rules are. What's left to do is in [`next steps.md`](next%20steps.md).
Setup, workflows and test hooks are in [`README.md`](README.md).*

Section numbers (§1.8, §7.10, …) are the ones `AUDIT.md`, `README.md` and
commit messages cite. Each one-line summary below replaces the old

**Vision:** procedurally generated racing across real-world places at 1:1
scale, built from open data, with a stylized "real places, storybook look".
**Distribution target:** a free public release on itch/GitHub.

---

## Standing decisions

1. **Art:** stylized, at about Fortnite/Marvel Rivals realism. Procedural
   assets should read as an intentional style. No chasing photorealism.
2. **Data:** nothing per-instance is ever stored (Taylor's rule). Store
   compact rasters/vectors (elevation, land cover, roads, climate). Everything
   visible is generated deterministically at runtime or in a rebuildable cache.
   Landmarks (§7.0 step 4) are the one allowed exception.
3. **Engine code:** C++ for vehicle physics, world gen and audio DSP.
   Blueprints for UI and game flow. Python for the offline pipeline and editor
   automation.
4. **Multiplayer:** "maybe someday". No replication work now, but keep choices
   replication-friendly when it costs nothing: deterministic world gen from
   seeds, tuning as data assets referenced by ID, and ghost recordings as
   portable artifacts.
5. **Cars:** CC0 low-poly packs. They must be license-clean, fit the art
   style, and be downloadable by the pipeline without editor sessions.
6. **Licensing/attribution (release requirement):** the credits screen must
   cover the full list in README.md § Data credits. Today it is generated by
   `pipeline/build_credits.py`.
7. **Scope (2026-07-27):** discrete mountain hot zones with fast travel
   between them, each with its own `origin_latlon` frame:
   - In scope: Cascades, Olympics, Sierra, the CA/OR/WA coast ranges, the US
     and Canadian Rockies, the BC Coast Mountains, Appalachians/Blue
     Ridge/Smokies, Adirondacks/Greens/Whites, Ozarks/Ouachitas, the Black
     Hills, all of Hawaii and all of Alaska.
   - Out of scope: the Great Plains, the Midwest, the Gulf coast and most of
     the desert Southwest.
   - Oahu is the debug testbed. The active area is `summit_county`.
   - NA-scale preprocessing and large-world-coordinates work are dropped.
8. **Weather:** random but realistic, driven by climate, biome and location.
   There is no weather picker; the season is chosen in the garage
   (decided 2026-09-25).

## Process rules

- **Verify pipeline changes offline:** backend scripts, plots, logs and the
  `verify_*` gates. Headless `Build.bat` compiles are fine. Live drive-tests
  are Taylor's.
- **`rebuild_area.py` `STAGES` order is load-bearing.** Walls come before
  terrain, and water comes before terrain. Never hand-run partial rebuilds out
  of order, because that bakes mixed generations. The GENID check catches
  stale tiles.
- **A full rebake must clear the pads-backup dirs:**
  - in `Masks/`: `_road_backup_junction_pads`, `_terrain_backup_junction_pads`
    and `_sidecar_backup_junction_pads`;
  - in `MasksV2/`: `_roadv3_backup_junction_pads`.
- **Downloads are cached in `data/cache/`** (§7.50). Delete a kind's dir to
  pull fresh data from the source.
- **Commit early**, even to a scratch branch. Three pipeline scripts were
  lost uncommitted in July.
- **Crash handling:** triage new UE crashes from `Saved/Crashes` before
  working around them. Keep automation headless.
- **QA flags and gotchas:**
  - `-game` renders need `-log`. Without `-game`, `AODMainMenu::BeginPlay`
    never runs.
  - `-odspawnx/y` needs `-odgo=N` or `-odpick`.
  - Scripted throttle needs both `-odrev=1` and `-odthr`. In PowerShell,
    quote it: `"-odthr=0.45"`.
  - `-odshot` causes its own hitch, so exclude those frames from hitch counts.
  - In Git Bash, use `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` for `/Game/...` args.
  - Spawn-test on real road stations, not tile centroids.
  - When collision misses a mesh that looks healthy, check winding first
    (§7.13).
- **Retired scripts:**
  - `pipeline/build_terrain_tiles.py` was deleted in the 7.0 pivot. It is
    replaced by `build_terrain_mesh_tiles.py`.
  - `pipeline/verify_road_fit.py` was lost, then rebuilt. It is current and
    gates road-vs-terrain fit plus poke-through (§7.46).
  - `road_mesh_common.sample_mesh_height` was reconstructed. It is now pinned
    by `pipeline/tests/test_sample_mesh_height.py`.
- **`scratchpad/` is git-ignored** (2026-09-29). It holds scratch output
  only.
  - The hand-run checks moved to `pipeline/checks/`: `esc_sim.py`,
    `verify_route_graph.py`, `verify_v3_ground_macro.py` and
    `capture_reconcile_pairs.py`.
  - The other old scratchpad files are gone. Code comments that cite
    `scratchpad/<file>` refer to them; recover one with
    `git show 8a882be:scratchpad/<file>`.

### 7.47 Road elevation rewrite: follow the ground (2026-09-29, d9affa5)

The drive-test report: roads were "not continuous with random dropoffs and
super wavy". The root cause was §7.11's one-sided min-envelope. It never lets
the road rise above the ground, and it applies a per-class grade cap. That
means the road sank under the terrain on any climb steeper than the cap:
- 7.8% of stations sat more than 5 m below the DEM;
- I-70 seg001 sat 15–45 m below it;
- Buffehr Creek sat 130 m below it.

The carve, the bridge floor and the junction pass then each patched those
spots separately, which produced the steps and the waves.

- **`follow_ground_profile`** (`road_mesh_common.py`) runs four steps:
  1. Smooth the DEM profile with a Gaussian (σ 4 stations).
  2. Relax each station's grade cap to the larger of the class cap and
     1.15× the 60 m trend slope, clipped to 22%.
  3. Take the mean of the cut envelope and the fill envelope.
  4. Smooth vertical curves with a Gaussian (σ 3).

  The relaxed caps are stored in `seg["max_grade_s"]` for the reconcile
  re-smooth.
- **Bridge clearance floor:** the lift ramps from each abutment at 6%
  (`BRIDGE_LIFT_RAMP_GRADE`) and is capped at 4 m. Bridges used to sit
  14–31 m proud of the ground.
- **`junction_agreement_pass` taper:** it now tapers the amount of the raise
  on top of the elevations from before the pass. Previously it laid an
  absolute line, which built a 103 m cliff on Willow Creek seg013.
- **Test:** `pipeline/tests/test_follow_ground_profile.py`. The old code
  fails it: it sinks more than 50 m and raises 35.8 m.
- **Backup:**
  `data/road_tiles/summit_county_test/road_stations.pre_follow_ground.json`.

| >5 m below | 7.77% | 0.31% |

### 7.48 Terrain pavement ceiling + 8-way parallel bake (2026-09-30, c5bc094/62d543c)

The drive-test report: the road sat below the terrain in many places.
- **`pavement_ceiling`** (`build_terrain_mesh_tiles.py`) caps the 4 corners
  of every cell a pavement probe lands in at the road's grade line minus a
  margin, and probes one cell into neighbouring tiles.
- **Near-tile margin is `HARD_CLAMP_MARGIN_M + 0.10`** (0.25 m). Martini
  simplification lifts the surface after the cap. An in-memory trial over 13
  I-70 tiles buried nothing at 0.15–0.45. The full 0.45 m floated the road a
  median 0.55 m, past the wrap lip on 7% of it.
- **Road-free far tiles skip the ceiling.** Otherwise the neighbour probes
  dragged their corners down and opened near/far seams of up to 49 m.
- **Bake runs 8 worker processes** (`BAKE_WORKERS`). Full terrain went from
  47 to 10.6 min. `verify_terrain_fit`'s pre-bake check and
  `build_forest_elevation` sample through the same pool.
- **Test:** `pipeline/tests/test_pavement_ceiling.py`.
- **Result:** 0 buried stations on near and coarse tiles. Near-tile float has
  a median of 0.36 m, with 11,699 stations over 0.7 m (3,521 before). Seams
  are unchanged: 378 edges over tolerance, all from the existing near/far
  skirts.

### 7.49 Junctions at interior nodes (2026-10-01)

The drive-test report: roads ended directly on top of other roads with no
junction.
- **Cause:** junction detection used `merge_way_chains`' degree, which counts
  only way ENDS. OSM joins a side street to a through way at one of the
  through way's interior nodes, which scored 1. That missed 1,664 T-junctions
  and 101 crossings.
- **Fix:** `junction_node_degree` counts 1 per way end and 2 per way passing
  through. Each segment carries `node_s`: every junction node along it and its
  arc length. The flatten blends at that arc length, on both sides for a
  through road.
- **Roads are not split at junctions.** Splitting would have dropped 1,119
  junction-to-junction pieces (53.5 km) under `MIN_SEGMENT_STATIONS`.
- **Test:** `pipeline/tests/test_junction_node_degree.py`.
- **Result:** junctions went from 495 to 1,771. Road ends on another road's
  pavement with no junction box went from 946 (286 over 1 m of height
  difference, worst +11 m at High Road / US 24) to 1 (0.13 m).
- **Full rebuild:** `verify_road_fit` PASSES (mean dev 0.18 m, max 4.24 m),
  with 0 poke-through. `verify_terrain_fit` fails only on Dennison Road
  station 184480 (3.95 m against 2.8 m).

### 7.50 Download cache (2026-10-01)

Every rebake re-downloaded the same data. Terrarium DEM tiles took about
1 s each and were fetched again by each stage that samples heights and by
each terrain worker.
- **`fetch_cache.py`** keeps every download under `data/cache/<kind>/`:
  `terrarium/` (via `terrain_common.fetch_terrarium_tile`), `usgs_imagery/`
  (`classify_biomes`), and `overpass/`. Overpass responses are keyed by query
  text, so the OSM fetch stages, water and parking all reuse them.
- **To refresh from the source,** delete that kind's dir, e.g.
  `data/cache/overpass/` to pick up OSM edits.
- **Test:** `pipeline/tests/test_fetch_cache.py`.
- **`sample_mesh_heights`** (`road_mesh_common.py`) is a batch version of
  `sample_mesh_height`. It bins triangles into a grid, gives bit-identical
  results, and is about 300× faster on a 75k-triangle near tile.
  `verify_forest_elevation_fit` went from over an hour to 10 s. The terrain
  pool's sampler and `verify_terrain_fit`'s shipped check use it too. Pinned
  by `test_batch_matches_scalar`.
- **`lakes_near`** (`build_terrain_mesh_tiles.py`) prefilters the 45k lake
  rings by bbox before barrier/guardrails/embankments' per-lake loops (about
  144M `bb.any()` calls each). Those stages went from 1000-1090 s to
  17-28 s, with byte-identical JSON. Test: `test_lakes_near.py`.
- **`sample_dem`** is vectorized (it was a per-point Python loop). Rebuilt
  terrain tiles are byte-identical to the shipped ones. Test:
  `test_sample_dem.py`.

### 7.51 Junction pads rerun for the §7.49 junctions (2026-10-01)

The drive-test report: a lot of roads don't truly connect. The pads hadn't
run since before §7.49, so 1,276 junctions had no pad.
- **Carve after pad-swallow.** The terrain regrade ran before the swallow grew
  the pads, so grown pads had raw terrain under them. It now runs after, and
  every pad also gets interior carve anchors on a 3 m grid at its plane
  height (`_pad_interior_points`).
- **Rim-flatten** (`flatten_to_pad_rims`) is a final grade pass over the
  FINAL pad shapes. Stations inside a pad sit on its surface, and approaches
  blend back from the rim over `TRANSITION_LEN_M`. Grown pads used to leave
  up to 3 m steps at the rim. Test: `pipeline/tests/test_flatten_to_pad_rims.py`.

  the weld potholes.
- **The station sidecar** is now patched with the snapped x/y as well as z.
  `verify_road_fit` and `verify_terrain_fit`'s shipped check probe those
  shipped positions (`_shipped_stations`). `verify_road_uv` tells pad
  vertices apart by u0 strictly inside (0, 1).
- **Tilted pads** (`fit_pad_tilts`, 2026-10-02). Every non-fused pad now
  sits on the plane through its roads' original grade (stations inside the

- **SF test area and the rim-blend rework** (2026-10-02). New area

- **Molded pads** (2026-10-02). A capped tilt plane can't follow a 25%
  street, so the rim crested/sagged where the road met it. `fit_pad_tilts`
  now also stores each road's miss from the plane (`j["mold"]`, one polyline
  per road). `pad_plane_z` adds that miss back with a smoothstep in the
  normalised radius: the plane at the centre, the road itself at the rim.
  - **Which pads.** Only pads whose tilt hit `PAD_TILT_MAX_GRADE`, and only
    when every road passes within `PAD_MOLD_MAX_CENTER_MISS_M` (0.5 m) of the
    plane at the centre.

      9,350 → 9,928 and steps 5,772 → 12,253.

  - **Mesh.** `_build_pad_fan` draws `PAD_MOLD_RINGS` (4) concentric rings

    stair-stepped.

      weld snap moved a station (up to ~11 m on SF), not the station's old
      `elev`.
    - The rim offset is measured at the first station outside the rim.
    - Competing approaches weight w/(1−w), so the pad whose rim is right
      there dominates.
    - Fill and gate-cover discs tilt onto the local road grade
      (`build_fill_disc`).
  - **Metric.** `connect_check` "steps" (a first difference over 0.25 m per
    1 m cell) counts grade on 25-30% streets. "Kinks" (3-cell second
    difference over 0.25 m) is the discontinuity measure.
